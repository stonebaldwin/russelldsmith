## Myth – VA Loans Have a 5 Acre Max

To understand the solution, let’s first discuss the problems. Primarily properties with more than a few acres can be difficult to finance. Actually, this is the case with most loans. One hurdle includes lenders having limits on the number of acres allowed with the home. For instance, lenders may set the limit at 5 acres. But even lenders which allow additional acreage, there could be another problem. The appraisal!

## VA Appraisal Excess Acreage

In order to finance a home with excess acreage, there must be sales with similar acreage size. So, a home being purchased with 20 acres should not be compared solely with sold homes that have 1 acre or less. These properties are not similar and more than likely would cause an appraisal review issue. On appraisals, this would be called a “line item adjustment”.  Basically, it makes the home unique. Typically, lenders do not like to lend on unique properties. Therefore, sales with excess acreage is key.

### Similar Comparable Sales is Key to VA Home Loan Acreage Limit

So, it is best to research the market up-front with the realtor plus discuss with your experienced VA lender. Determine if there are recent sales of homes with similar acres. Although, the sales do not have to match exactly.  But if there are similar sales, then that is where VA farm residence loans could finance that dream property. So contrary to belief, VA will lend on properties with more than 5 acres. Technically, there’s not a VA home loan acreage limit. Check out a success story below!

## VA Guideline for Farm Residences – VA Home Loan Acreage Limit

So, can you buy a home with a lot of land? Actually, VA loan guidelines say “Yes”. Specifically, chapter 11 of the VA handbook (what VA lenders use as reference) explains how farm residences are treated. Although, you will see that VA allows buying a farm residence, still many lenders shy away from this guideline. But don’t fault lenders as homes like this are fairly unique. Thus, the issue. Fortunately, we do follow the VA guidelines for stick built homes and excess acreage.

## VA Loans Do Not Have an Acreage Limit

First of all, VA loans are for residential purposes and not for business loans. But, VA farm loans allow Veterans to use their VA eligibility to purchase a farm on which there is a farm residence. In other words, it must be a primary residence. Most importantly, _**VA does not set a limit on the number of acres which the property may have.**_

### VA Farm Residences Appraisal Requirements

A VA appraisal should not be a problem if there is a sufficient amount of similar properties sold in the area. Additionally, VA land guidelines state farm characteristics such as barns, sheds, corrals, stables, and pastures are acceptable for residential use. Yet, the VA appraisal may not include livestock, crops, farm equipment, or supplies in the valuation.

### VA Appraised Value of Barns, Sheds, Corrals, Stables, & Pastures

Any of these farming features’ value must be solely based on residential purposes. For instance, stables may be expected to bring in a certain income or may cost a lot to build. But, a VA appraisal may not take farming business valuation into consideration. Rather, the appraiser would consider the fair market value for residential purposes only. A building would probably be counted as storage or garage rather than a business purpose.
