## Brunswick County’s Low Property Tax Rate:

A recent [**WalletHub article lists North Carolina having the 19th lowest property taxes in the U.S.**](https://wallethub.com/edu/states-with-the-highest-and-lowest-property-taxes/11585/) .  Northern buyers are so surprised by how much their property taxes will decrease just by moving to Brunswick County.  Per the [NC Department of Revenue](http://www.dornc.com/), the Brunswick County property tax rate is only .4850 per $100 in tax valuation.  Therefore it is one of North Carolina’s lowest tax rates for 2015-2016.  The next tax revaluation is scheduled for 2019 in Brunswick County which means the county tax office will determine the new tax value for each property.  The property tax rate will be multiplied times the tax rate to determine the property tax.

## Brunswick County Golf Courses

**Play With a “Local Rate” at so Many Amazing Brunswick County Golf Courses!**

Brunswick County quiet lies across the state line from [Myrtle Beach SC](http://www.myrtlebeach.com/).  Many claim Myrtle Beach to be the “Golf ![Brunswick County NC golf courses](/images/blog/brunswick-county-nc/golf-sunset-300x212.png)Capital of the World”.  Well, Brunswick County also offers championship caliber golf courses at affordable pricing.  On top of living next to so many courses, you can even get a resident discount for most courses in Brunswick County.  This even applies to many courses across the line in South Carolina!  Additionally, if you were to play a different course every weekend, it would take you 2 1/2 years to play the approximately 30 popular courses in Brunswick County and over 100 in the Myrtle Beach area!

If golfing is too tough for you then, you can also cross the line to the “Mini-golf capital of the world, Myrtle Beach, SC”.  Some of the more popular golf courses in Brunswick County include Magnolia Greens, Oyster Bay Golf Links, Bald Head Island Club, Cape Fear National at Brunswick Forest, Carolina National Golf Club, Crow Creek, The Pearl, Sandpiper Bay, Sea Trail Resort, Lockwood Folly Country Club, Farmstead Golf Links, and many more.

## Create Lasting Memories Under the Sun on Brunswick County’s Sandy Beaches

![Brunswick County NC beaches](/images/blog/brunswick-county-nc/beach-mailbox-300x200.jpg)

Whether you are looking for a weekend getaway or a place to grow your roots for life, the Brunswick County coastal beach towns offer so much for you to enjoy.  The nice thing about these beaches is that they are not the overcrowded, over-commercialized beaches variety.  So, you can experience the seclusion of Bald Head Island, catch the [**Oyster Festival at Ocean Isle Beach**](http://ncoysterfestival.com/), watch the shrimp boats at Holden Beach, or watch the sea turtles journey from their nest to the ocean in Oak Island.  Other beaches and coastal towns include Southport, Holden Beach, Calabash (Seafood Capital of the World), Shallotte, Leland, Sunset Beach, Caswell Beach, plus others.  What’s even better is that you can live anywhere in Brunswick County and be within 30 minutes of all of this (plus a little longer to take the boat ride over to Bald Head Island).

## So You Want Some Good Seafood?  Famous Calabash Seafood Style in Brunswick County!

First of all, if we are going to talk seafood.  So we have to mention Calabash which proudly holds the title of “Seafood Capital of the World”.  It is because of the world famous signature seafood preparation – Calabash style.  Calabash Style is when shrimp, oysters, clams, and fish are coated in a light batter.  Then they are quickly fried to lock in their freshness.  Make sure that you experience the famous Annual North Carolina Oyster Festival!  It occurs in October of each year in Ocean Isle Beach.![Brunswick County NC best popular dining and restaurants](/images/blog/brunswick-county-nc/dining-solo-300x200.jpg)

Popular [restaurants](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Restaurants-g48995-Calabash_North_Carolina.html) we like include Wing & Fish Company in Shallotte and Twin Lakes Seafood in Sunset Beach.  Additionally, Russell’s Place for breakfast in Oak Island, Fishy Fishy Café in Southport, Ella’s – Calabash Seafood Hut – Beck’s or Capt Nance’s in Calabash, and Mojo’s on the Harbor in Bald Head Island.  You can do like the golf thing and eat in a different restaurant every week for years.  And that doesn’t even count MYRTLE BEACH restaurants just across the NC/SC line!

Wow, that’s a lot of good reasons to buy in or at least vacation in Brunswick County, NC!  But there are so many other things to enjoy.  These include freshwater and ocean fishing, entertainment, shopping, and water sports.
