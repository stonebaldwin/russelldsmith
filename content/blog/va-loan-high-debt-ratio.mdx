## First Time VA Loan Buyer Options

## Under 640 score, over 55% debt ratio, [VA home loan](/va-loans/) purchase that needs to close in 33 days?  No way?  Yes, Way!!

So often there are times when someone has some hurdles to overcome when purchasing a home and our team is one that will figure out a way to make the loan work if it can be.  We were just recently faced with the following scenario:

-   620 credit score
-   57% debt ratio
-   No money down
-   Active duty service member
-   First time buyer
-   Need to close in 33 days

The buyer provided us with everything requested up-front which allowed us to obtain underwriting approval in plenty of time prior to closing to make everyone feel at ease.  We were able to prove a rent history from the buyer which was paid on time and the mortgage payment is only $80 more per month than the rent which was a huge compensating factor.  Also, we were able to figure a little known way to increase the service member’s nontaxable income to get the debt ratio lowered to a better level.

## The end result is that we received the final approval for this [**VA**](/va-loans/) home loan purchase and the file is closing on-time with the closing disclosure to the buyer over 3 days prior to closing!
