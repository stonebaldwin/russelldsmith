## Moore County Places 3 in the “Niche 2015 Best Towns to Buy a House in North Carolina”

Niche.com provides “best of” lists in the U.S. and Moore County hit a home run by being the only NC county to place 3 towns in the top 25 best towns to buy a home in NC.  Pinehurst ranked highest at #5 followed by Aberdeen at #19, and Southern Pines at #25.  To comprise the list, they took into account the home values, property taxes, housing costs, the age of new home buyers, and other factors.  Niche says “A high ranking indicates that a town attracts residents with a good housing market where property taxes and housing costs are in line with value”.  [**See the actual methodology Niche used for ranking this list**](https://local.niche.com/rankings/towns/best-places-to-buy-a-house/methodology/).

## Moore County’s Very Low Property Tax Rate:

A recent [**WalletHub article lists North Carolina having the 19th lowest property taxes in the ![Moore County NC Demographics](/images/blog/moore-county-nc/moore-county-census-bureau-2015-300x213.gif)U.S.**](https://wallethub.com/edu/states-with-the-highest-and-lowest-property-taxes/11585/) and this is why we hear so many moving from the northern states so surprised by how much their property taxes will decrease when they move here.  Per the NC Department of Revenue, the Moore County property tax rate is only .4650 per $100 in tax valuation which is one of North Carolina’s lowest tax rates for 2015-2016 and is easily the lowest property tax rate when compared to the 9 surrounding counties.  So even if you work in a neighboring county, it can make sense to own in Moore County for low property taxes.  The next tax revaluation is scheduled for 2019 in Moore County which means the county tax office will determine the new tax value for each property.  The property tax rate will be multiplied times the tax rate to determine the property tax you will pay.

## Places to Eat and Lots of Activities for Residents & Visitors

If you thought Moore County is only about golf, you couldn’t be more wrong!  There are so many outdoor activities and when you are done with that, you can experience some unbelievable and unique restaurants.

## **Dining Hot Spots – Some of the best places you never heard of!**

If you have a hankering for barbeque and brisket, you need to try [**Pik n Pig**](http://www.pik-n-pig.com/) in Carthage which has 3 ![Moore County Best places to dine](/images/blog/moore-county-nc/dining-pana-1-300x119.png)generations of family experience in giving you the best southern dining experience.  Pinehurst has several great must eats including [**Elliott’s on Linden**](http://www.elliottsonlinden.com/), [**Drum & Quill Publ****ic House**](http://www.drumandquill.com/) with a friendly pub atmosphere, and a unique pizza diner called [**Pinehurst Pizza**](http://pinehurstpizza.com/).  Southern Pines boasts eateries such as **195 Eclectic Cuisine** that is off the beaten path but worth the find, and then there is [**Ashten’s**](http://www.ashtens.com/) that boasts a fine wine collection, a popular downtown pub hangout, and award winning dishes.  There are many more places to check out.  A popular watering hole and seafood grill is the [**Full Moon Oyster Bar**](http://www.fullmoonoysterbar.com/) in Clemmons, Southern Pines, and other NC locations.  Favorites include homemade recipes, fresh oyster oysters, and other shellfish delights.

## **Things to do –** **Indoor or Outdoor Activities**

Moore County has plenty to do inside, but it really excels in the outdoor activities and it even has a fun place for the pets in Pooch Park in the Pines which is a fully fenced-in dog park.  Do you like to walk or bike trails?  Try the 5.8 mile (of the planned 24 miles) Pinehurst Greenway System, Sandhills Horticultural Gardens which can keep you busy for hours in the beautiful scenery, Southern Pines Greenway System, Aberdeen Lake Park, Lake Luke Marion, Hillcrest Park, Weymouth Woods, and the All American Trail (a must visit!).  Let’s say you want to try something different…There are 3 great Disc Golf courses which includes wooded and beautiful course catering to all skill levels.

## **Want to come inside, take a stroll in town, or enjoy some nightlife?**

Check out Willie’s Lunch Box for Karaoke and live music, Triangle Wine Company offers themed Tap Takeovers and wine tastings, and [**Sunrise Theater**](http://www.sunrisetheater.com/) where you can enjoy a cold beer or wine while watching a movie, opera, or ballet.  Enjoy a stroll in historic [**Pinehurst Village**](http://www.pinehurst.com/activities/village-at-pinehurst/).  See what Donald Ross and the Tuft family saw back in 1895.  Whether a sandwich at the Village Deli or rocking on the porch of the [**Pinecrest Inn Pinehurst**](http://www.pinecrestinnpinehurst.com/) is a storied place with something for everyone.  If you want to pick up the pace a bit, just go a few miles over to downtown Southern Pines and see all of the happenings on Broad Street.  A railroad town, visit all of the shops, restaurants, microbrews, and nightlife on both sides of the tracks.  Oh ok, we know many of you want to know about the golf, so here you go!

## World Renowned Pinehurst Golf – Play any of the 9 area courses

![Pinehurst golf courses 1 - 9](/images/blog/moore-county-nc/2478800216_359ca747f2_b-300x201.jpg)

Famous Pinehurst has hosted the best golfers in the world and the good thing is you too can walk through the same pine tree lined courses which they have.  Pinehurst has actually been the site of more championships than any other golf course in the country including the U.S. Open and Women’s U.S. Open in 2014.  Pinehurst offers some very affordable golf retreat packages where you can experience the 9 famous courses.

-   No. 1 which was originally opened in 1898!
-   World Championship level golf on No. 2 which hosts the U.S. Open, designed in 1907
-    No. 3 that has the tiny greens built in 1910
-   No. 4, the newest course was designed by Tom Fazio in 2000
-   No. 5 opened in 1961 and one of Pinehurst’s prettiest holes, par 3 15th, is on this course
-   No. 6 is a little further from the original 5 & “Nestled amid the quiet pines”
-   No. 7 is the second longest course and has a lot of color during your 18 holes
-   No. 8 is described by Pinehurst as combining the classical concepts with the whimsical snarls
-   No. 9, a classic Jack Nicklaus design, Pinehurst says it “will test your mind and your true golfing ability”
