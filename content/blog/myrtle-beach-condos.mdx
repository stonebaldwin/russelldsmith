Did you know that a [Veteran](https://www.va.gov/) can purchase a condo as a primary residence at the beach?  VA approved myrtle beach condos are plentiful and offer a great, relaxing lifestyle.  As of today, there are actually 49 approved condominium complexes in Horry County, SC.  Plus this number should grow.  The VA approved condos are spread throughout Myrtle Beach, North Myrtle Beach, Little River, Longs, Surfside Beach, and Murrells Inlet, SC.

[Search for VA Approved Condos](https://vip.vba.va.gov/portal/VBAH/VBAHome/condopudsearch)
