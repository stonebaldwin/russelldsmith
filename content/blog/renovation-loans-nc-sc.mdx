## How FHA 203k Renovation Loans Work

Most are familiar with an [**FHA loan**](/fha-loans/).  It is a low down payment loan that is forgiving on credit among other ![renovation loans for a purchase rehab](/images/blog/renovation-loans-nc-sc/renovation-comparison-staircase-300x194.jpg)guidelines.  Furthermore, what most don’t realize is that there is a subprogram called an FHA 203k which allows for improvements.  These may be financed into a purchase or refinance loan.  In addition, benefits of the loan include:

-   Down payment is 3.5%
-   Use the “As Completed” appraised value
-   Certain costs for the renovation may be in the loan
-   Work may be completed after closing

Initially, we mentioned examples of home improvements allowed under this program.  In addition, the FHA 203k loan is for primary residences only.  So if buying a second home or rental property, we are not able to use this product. Finally, there is a requirement of a 660 minimum credit score.

## How the FHA 203k Renovation Loan Process Works

1.  First, the borrower applies for prequalification
2.  A property is identified![renovation loans for fixer upper purchase](/images/blog/renovation-loans-nc-sc/houseplan-papers-300x215.jpg)
3.  Borrower receives bid for improvements
4.  Contractor completes contractor approval documentation
5.  HUD Consultant chosen and paid by borrower (If a Full 203k which is a higher level of improvements)
6.  Appraisal is ordered after the bids are provided
7.  Appraiser completes the appraisal using the “as completed” value after improvements
8.  Additional repairs required by the appraiser are added to the bid
9.  Submit the loan to underwriting
10.  Loan closes after all loan conditions are completed
11.  Then, Renovation may commence after closing
12.  Contractor is paid
13.  Dream home accomplished!
