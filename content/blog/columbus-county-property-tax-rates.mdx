Columbus County is the 3rd largest county in the state of North Carolina.  Also it’s largest town and county seat is Whiteville, NC.  So if looking to buy here, you should look up the Columbus County property tax rates.  In addition to knowing the actual taxes, often the taxes are included in the mortgage payment.  Therefore knowing the taxes will help someone buying a home here to know the total mortgage payment.

## Columbus County Property Tax Rates

To figure property taxes on a property in Columbus County, there is are some options.  One option is to look up the property tax rates for the particular property.
