## Low Credit Scores Can Get Approved


Today there are many prospective homeowners with low credit scores, higher debt to income ratios, or limited credit experience.  This can make it difficult to obtain a mortgage loan to purchase a home.  Potential homebuyers do need to prove their ability to pay a mortgage on-time in order to get a mortgage loan approval.  We have investors for [FHA loans](/fha-loans/) that will lend to buyers with these characteristics.

## Actual examples of recent [FHA purchase](/fha-loans/) closings

-   [FHA loan](/fha-loans/) with a 581 middle credit score.  So buyer was able to close with less than a 600 credit score but had other compensating factors.  It is difficult to get approved under 600 though
-   [FHA loan](/fha-loans/) with a 55% debt ratio.  The buyer had a higher debt ratio but had compensating factors including a high credit score, credit experience over a long period of time, and a solid income
-   [FHA loan](/fha-loans/) with very limited credit.  If we are able to get an automated pre-approval for someone that may only have 1 or 2 items reporting on their credit report, we have investors that we could close an [FHA loan](/fha-loans/) for.  If a borrower has very limited credit like this, it needs to be good credit and not 1 good item with several collections for instance and it helps if the borrower is able to prove a good rent payment history.

Keep in mind that [FHA loans](/fha-loans/) do have some leniency in their guidelines but it always helps to have the best credit possible to increase the chance of loan approval and to have more loan options to choose from.  To learn more about FHA loans, [click here](/fha-loans/).
