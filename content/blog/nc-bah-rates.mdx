## 2018 BAH Rates Increase

Are you changing duty stations and relocating to a North Carolina base?  Then, knowing the NC BAH rates will help military buyers budget for a house.  BAH is a very key part of U.S. service members’ pay because it helps pay a housing payment.  Furthermore, this part of the LES is nontaxable, which helps the bottom line a lot.  Additionally, since BAH is nontaxable, we can gross up this income to lower a debt ratio.  Lowering the debt ratio with this trick, aids in qualifying for a [**VA home loan**](/va-loans/).  [**Learn how**](/blog/did-you-know-that-some-forms-of-nontaxable-income-can-be-grossed-up/).![What are the 2016 BAH rates for NC?](/images/blog/nc-bah-rates/militaryhousing-allowance-300x225.jpg)
