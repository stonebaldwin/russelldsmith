Whether buying a principal residence or converting a [**vacation**](/blog/so-many-ways-besides-cash-to-buy-a-second-home-at-the-beach-or-mountains/) / [**rental home**](/blog/rental-property/) to a primary, apply for the exemption!  Horry County offers a huge property tax discount for living in the home.  So make sure you take advantage of this tax break immediately.

In order to receive the **Horry County SC Residential Exemption**, homeowners must complete the Legal Residence Special Assessment Application.  We provide the application link further down this article.  This form must be given to the Horry County Tax Assessor’s office.

## How Does the Residential Exemption Work?

First of all, the easiest way to figure the Horry County property taxes would be to look them up.  Search [Horry County Land Records](http://www.horrycounty.org/OnlineServices/LandRecords) for the property.  If the property already shows a 4% tax rate, then there are no other calculations necessary.  BUT, if there is a homestead exemption, add back this figure if not a senior.  A Homestead Exemption is for seniors living in the Horry County home.  Furthermore, the [Horry County GIS](http://www.horrycounty.org/gis/parcels/) site provides lots of helpful information to potential and current residents.

Additionally, there is a huge benefit for 100% service connected [disabled veterans](https://www.va.gov/)!  It is actually possible to have no property taxes!  Plus [Veterans](http://www.military.com/benefits) could use a [VA home loan](/va-loans/) for potentially a money down purchase as well.  Learn how this [SC Property Tax Exemption works for Veterans](/blog/sc-property-tax-exemption/).

In Horry County SC, the property taxes are determined by multiplying a few items.  Just multiply _Fair Market Value_ times _Assessment Ratio_ times the _Millage Rate_.

### How to Calculate Property Taxes with the Residential Exemption

So let’s figure how to use the calculations to determine property taxes after the discount.  In this example, we are going to use $125,000 as a tax value.  Plus we will assume the property reports as a non-owner occupied residence in Horry County Land Records.  Therefore we need to recalculate the tax using the residential exemption calculations.

## Horry County SC Residential Exemption Example

Fair Market Value$125,000 Assessment RatioX 4% Assessed Value$5000 Countywide Millage RateX .21 Taxes Before Discount$1,050 Exemption Tax CreditX .12 Exemption Credit Figure- $600 Total Taxes with Discount$450

Keep in mind that this formula will give you a close figure and doesn’t take all factors into account.  Each property could be a little different based on district and more.  If you have located a specific property, look it up first in Horry County land records.  If the 6% tax rate is used, use the above example to figure the approximate lower primary residence taxes.
