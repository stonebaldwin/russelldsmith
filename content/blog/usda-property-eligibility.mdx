## USDA Property Eligibility – Searching for areas in general

![USDA property eligibility - Zoom in to see USDA eligible and ineligible areas](/images/blog/usda-property-eligibility/usda-zoom-in-feature.png)

Zoom in to see USDA eligible and ineligible areas

-    [Click here for property eligibility search](https://eligibility.sc.egov.usda.gov/eligibility/welcomeAction.do) 
-   Go to “Single Family Housing Guaranteed” on the left
-   Click “Accept”
-   Grab the area on the map you want to zoom into and move it to the middle of your screen
-   Then, scroll your mouse or use the zoom bar to the left to zoom into your area
-   Browse the area.  Especially important for properties near the borderline.  For instance, pay attention to which side of the road is eligible on these borders.
