## Sellars Cove Condos, VA and USDA Financing Approved

![Sellars Cove Condos in Wilmington are VA and USDA approved condos](/images/blog/sellars-cove-condos-va-loans/sellars-cove-300x224.jpg)

Sellars Cove Condos, which are located at Condo Club Drive, Wilmington, NC 28412, are currently approved for [VA](/va-loans/) & [USDA](/usda-loans/) financing.  Many are not aware that many condos are more difficult to finance than regular homes.  Though, our team  has experience with lending for Sellars Cove Condos for [VA](/va-loans/) & [USDA](/usda-loans/) loans.  Sellars Cove condos are built in 2008 with brick, stone, and vinyl exterior.  Amenities include a community pool, elevators, and double paned windows.

Additionally, living here has benefits of being near shopping, restaurants, and Beau Rivage golf course with a short drive to Carolina Beach and downtown Wilmington.

-   Contact us first for a loan pre-approval for either a [VA](/va-loans/), [USDA](/usda-loans/), and possibly a conventional mortgage
-   It is possible to get 100% financing on a condo in Sellars Cove with [VA](/va-loans/) and [USDA](/usda-loans/)
-   Once you have a pre-approval letter from our team, you can then ask your realtor to show you the property(ies) and make an offer
