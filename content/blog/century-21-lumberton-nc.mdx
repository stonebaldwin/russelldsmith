Century 21 has been a cornerstone of the Lumberton community for quite a long time.  Although, it is the members of the [Century 21 Lumberton NC](http://c21therealestatecenter.com/) office that make it special.  Recently the C21 family held their annual awards breakfast and recognized top agents, support staff, and new team members.  These [C21 real estate agents](http://c21therealestatecenter.com/our-agents) and support staff serve Robeson County and surrounding areas with their unsurpassed expertise.  But what is really nice to see is how involved their family is in civic, church, and charitable organizations within Robeson County.  A focal point discussed was the amount of volunteering during Hurricane Matthew.
The annual awards breakfast was led by Elliott Williamson, broker in charge and owner.  The event was held at Abigail’s Tea Room located at 4531 Fayetteville Rd, Lumberton.

My favorite quote of the morning came from the top producing real estate agent.  Plus it wasn’t about how many homes closed or how much money someone made.  It was about friendships and service!

> “Friends and friendships, Do what you can, when you can.” Leroy Rising

## Century 21 Lumberton NC Experience is Unbelievable!!

Real estate agents working in the [C21 Real Estate Center](http://c21therealestatecenter.com/) office combine for an incredible 285 years of C21 service.  You would be hard pressed to find a large real estate firm in a big city to even have such experience.  As the years of service among the Realtors were named, it was just “Wow!”  Even some of the C21 agents that have the least years with C21 have years of experience.  They were just with a different agency.

### C21 Top 100 Producers in North Carolina

Each year Century 21 honors the top real estate agents in each state.  This Century 21 Lumberton NC office placed 4 agents in the top 100.  These agents included Leroy Rising, Linda Metzger, Amy Smith, and Georgia Stamper.

## Century 21 Lumberton NC By the Numbers

Although 2016 was a year marred by Hurricane Matthew, C21 The Real Estate Center office still had a wonderful year.  Basically because of the storm and flood damage, not a lot of real estate closed in the county.  This not only affected C21, but also buyers, sellers, and others that depend on the real estate market.  During the awards presentation, both the sales and rental departments were praised for their hard work.

### Century 21 Rental Homes Department Excels

Not only does C21 sell homes, but they manage a lot of rental properties!  520 properties under rentals in 2016 is quite an impressive number.  Among those rentals, there was $2.9 million in rental payments received.  With these numbers, one can see that the rental market is important for the local residents.  Real estate investors benefit from the property management offered by C21’s rental department.  Additionally Lumberton tenants enjoy a high level of customer service and lots of rental home options.
