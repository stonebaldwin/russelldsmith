##  2016 Military Housing (BAH) Rates for Service Members Increased on average 3.4% from 2015

Are you changing duty stations and relocating to a South Carolina base? Then, knowing the SC BAH rates / income will help military buyers budget for a house. BAH is a key part of U.S. service members’ pay because it helps pay a housing payment. Furthermore, this part of the LES is nontaxable, which helps the bottom line a lot. Additionally, since BAH is non-taxable, we can gross up this income to lower a debt ratio. So lowering the debt ratio with this trick, aids in qualifying for a [**VA home loan**](/va-loans/).  [**Learn how**](/blog/did-you-know-that-some-forms-of-nontaxable-income-can-be-grossed-up/).
