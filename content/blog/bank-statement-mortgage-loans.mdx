Homeownership, owning your business, and living life to the fullest are 3 of the most popular answers to the American dream.  One could see why many desire to own a business.  Successful business owners enjoy a sense of pride in building something that supports their employees and provides valuable services.  Yet, the self employed also have a business to be their own boss and control their lifestyle.  Lifestyle includes time, finances, and even [homeownership](https://www.zillow.com/).  But, over the years [homeownership](/blog/buy-a-house/) was a challenge for business owners because of stringent tax returns income requirements.  That is where bank statement mortgage loans come in handy.

## NO Paystubs, No Tax Returns, No W2s Required

Wait!  Self employed business owners have to provide 2 years of [tax returns](https://www.irs.gov/), k1’s, w2’s, and a pint of blood right?  That is the beauty of the bank statement mortgage loans.  For [self employed](/blog/self-employed-mortgage/) borrowers with a business in existence for at least 2 years, these documents are not required here.  Unlike [FHA](https://portal.hud.gov/hudportal/HUD), Fannie, [Freddie](http://www.freddiemac.com/), or [VA](https://www.ebenefits.va.gov/ebenefits/homepage), there are no tax returns required. So, this loan program is a great mortgage alternative.  Income documentation solutions include:

-   12 months personal bank statements
-   24 months personal bank statements
-   24 months business bank statements

## Bank Statement Mortgage Loans Allow Low Credit Scores

Not only do bank statement mortgage loans offer income documentation alternatives, but they also provide credit flexibility as well.  Life happens sometimes and those life situations may affect [credit scores](/blog/how-to-have-perfect-walk-on-water-qualifies-for-the-best-rate-there-is-credit/) temporarily.  Credit situations such as foreclosure, short sale, or bankruptcy act as a speed bump to getting mortgage approval.  But, bank statement mortgage loans allow as short as 12 – 24 months since the event!  Credit scores may even be as low as 580, but re-established credit including rent or mortgage payments are important.
