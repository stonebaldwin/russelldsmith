USDA announces increase in the USDA guarantee or funding fee effective 10/1/2015 to 2.75%

For buyers, [USDA](/usda-loans/) mortgage loans are so popular (especially in NC, SC, and Virginia) because they allow first time or subsequent homebuyers to purchase a primary residence with no money down and with low monthly payments.

### **The USDA National Office just announced on June 16, 2015 that effective with guaranteed loan obligations on or after October 1, 2015 through September 30, 2016, the up-front [USDA](/usda-loans/) guarantee fee is increasing from 2.0% to 2.75%**.

This [USDA](/usda-loans/) up-front fee is financed on top of the base loan size just as it is done for [VA](/va-loans/) and [FHA](/fha-loans/) loans so the change does not require the buyer to bring additional money to closing but it will make their payment a few dollars more per month.  The Annual Fee will remain at .50% which is factored into the monthly payment.  If you are looking to purchase soon & would use a [USDA](/usda-loans/) loan, let’s get the [USDA](/usda-loans/) commitment prior to October 1!  [Learn how the USDA Annual Fee is a little different from traditional PMI here](/blog/when-does-pmi-stop-on-fha-usda-and-conventional-mortgage-loans/).

The Up-Front [USDA](/usda-loans/) guarantee fee increase does also apply to [USDA Streamline or Pilot Refinances](/blog/usda-rural-development-mortgage-loan-pilot-streamline-refinance-available-without-appraisal/).

The official [USDA Rural Development](/usda-loans/) unnumbered letter dated 6/16/2015 “Upfront Guarantee Fee and Annual Fee Schedule Effective October 1, 2015” can be [read here](http://www.rd.usda.gov/publications/regulations-guidelines/unnumbered-letters) and is available under “June 2015” on this page.  Keep in mind that a loan is obligated when the mortgage lender has fully underwritten the file, [USDA](/usda-loans/) has approved a complete loan package, and [USDA](/usda-loans/) has issued form RD 3555-18 which is the “Conditional Commitment for Single Family Housing Loan Guarantee” to the lender.
