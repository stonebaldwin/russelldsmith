## Brunswick County USDA Loan Benefits

USDA loans are split into two main categories:  Direct and Guaranteed Loans.  [USDA Direct loans](https://www.rd.usda.gov/) are for low income and provide![brunswick county usda eligible](/images/blog/brunswick-county-usda/usda-logo-on-for-sale-sign-1.jpg) opportunity for subsidized housing payments.  Only local USDA offices offer this option. Although, this article discusses the USDA Guaranteed home loan offered through mortgage lenders.  USDA Rural Development Guaranteed loans offer 100% financing at a low 30 year fixed interest rate.  Actually, that is the only term offered. So buyers, don’t worry about adjustable rates or payments.  Additionally, couple USDA loans with very low [Brunswick County property taxes](/blog/brunswick-county-nc/), then you get very affordable living.

When buyers put down less than 20% down payment, mortgage insurance is usually required.  Mortgage insurance is in the form of up-front, monthly, or both.  USDA loans have performed well enough so that it is able to offer one of the best options available.  The [up-front funding fee](/blog/2016-2017-usda-funding-fee/) is only 1% of the amount financed.  Additionally, it is automatically financed in the total loan amount.  Furthermore the mortgage insurance rate, which USDA calls an Annual Fee, is only .35%.  Compare USDA with other popular mortgage fees below.  All figures assume a 30 year fixed term and minimum down payment required for each program.

## Brunswick County USDA Income Limits are Liberal

Yes, there is an income limit.  But USDA household income limits are very liberal.  Keep in mind, all household member income must be factored in the USDA household income limit.  So even if there is a 19 year old working a part time job while in the home, it must be counted.  It does not mean all household members need to be on the mortgage or deed.  This is just for calculating maximum household income.

### Brunswick County USDA Income Limits 2018-2019

### 1 – 4 Person Household = $82,700

### 5 + Person Household = $109,150

Plus there are other ways to exceed these USDA income limits.  One way to exceed the limit is based on number of children under 18 years old.  The limit may be increased by $480 for each child. So if there are 4 people in the home and 2 are children under 18, the income limit is $83,660.  Calculate by adding $82,700 plus $960 (2 children x $480). Another way is adding documented childcare expenses such as daycare.  Therefore if a family pays $6000 per year in daycare, the income limit may be increased by this amount.  Finally, [medical expenses](https://www.healthcare.gov/) for disabled household members could potentially help get within the income limits.  Learn more details in our article “[How to exceed the USDA Household Income Limits](/blog/how-to-exceed-the-usda-household-income-limits/)“.  So as you can tell, USDA safely allows middle income America to buy with no money down!
