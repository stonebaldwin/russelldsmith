The Federal Housing Administration has announced the [FHA](https://portal.hud.gov/hudportal/HUD) loan size limits for 2019 in all areas of each state.  The most popular level is for single family residences.  Most of the NC FHA loan size limits increased to $314,827.  But there are several [NC counties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special:Search?search=nc+counties&go=Go&searchToken=ayrbhvsq8l7yxrdvq1upqfdcv) which have higher FHA loan size limits.  These high balance NC counties helps more buyers afford a home with a 3.5% down payment.

FHA loans are known for flexible guidelines, affordable monthly payments, and low cash to close.  These advantages make it easier to buy a [home](http://www.realtor.com/) with a low down payment.  Many unknown, but valuable features include:

1.  [Gift](/blog/gift-funds-fha/), down payment assistance, and other flexible down payment sources
2.  55% or even higher debt ratios allowed
3.  [Non-occupying coborrowers](/blog/problems-with-debt-ratio-fha-and-conventional-programs-that-allow-co-borrowers-that-dont-live-in-the-property/)
4.  As low as 600 credit scores
5.  Previous foreclosure, bankruptcy, or short sale allowed
6.  [1 year commission income](/blog/commission-income-for-fha-loans/) often ok
7.  [Seller paid costs up to 6% of sales price](/blog/maximum-seller-paid-costs/)
8.  Low down payment [condo loans](/blog/fha-approved-condos-search/)
9.  [Manufactured homes](/blog/doublewide-manufactured-homes/) and [modular home](/blog/on-frame-off-frame-modulars/) financing
10.  [Renovation loans for home improvements](/blog/renovation-loans-nc-sc/)

[Current FHA Guidelines Help More Buyers Qualify](/blog/fha-guidelines/)

So if using an FHA loan, check out these limits.  Then compare your approximate loan amount with the corresponding FHA loan size limits.

Know Your NC FHA Loan Limits

## Standard 2019 NC FHA Loan Size Limits:

-   One-Family Homes = $314,827
-   Two -Family Homes = $403,125
-   Three-Family Homes = $487,250
-   Four-Family Homes = $605,525
