## Facebook Lists is a key tool for growing a business

![Facebook lists for realtors and builders](/images/blog/realtor-builder-marketing-tip-facebook-lists/social-media-grads.jpg)

## Using Facebook Lists can be a great way to talk directly with your clients

Facebook can be your greatest ally in your marketing plan if used the right way.  So much of the world is on Facebook and there are so many ways to market for free or at a very low cost on there.  First, in order to create an efficient and effective Facebook marketing plan, you should organize your contacts to build your foundation.  This key point about creating and organizing Facebook Lists will help you stay organized in our marketing efforts and accomplish this important task.  So let’s dive on in!

## How to Create a New Facebook Lists to Organize Your Friends

-   Scroll down to friends on the left side of your news feed
-   Hover over “Friends” and click on “More”
-   Click on “Create List”
-   Name your list
-   Add the names you want to put in this particular list
-   Click “Create”

You can continue to add or remove friends from the lists you create. Creating Facebook lists is great way to easily talk with and interact with your target audience.  For instance, you might want to create lists, such as…

-   Previous buyers
-   Prospective buyers
-   Previous sellers
-   Accountants
-   Financial Advisors
-   Realtors
-   Builders
