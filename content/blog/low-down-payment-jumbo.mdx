## Low down payment jumbo loans

Jumbo mortgage loans over recent years have been more conservative than other loans.  Although we have some low down payment solutions for today’s luxury home buyer. The improvement in the US economy has really helped increase the number of sales of the higher priced luxury homes. For single family primary residences (1 unit only), we have 10% down options for mortgage loans over $424,100. Of course, in order to qualify for the low down payment jumbo loans, borrowers need to have solid 700+ credit scores and sufficient asset reserves after closing.

#### **Options for the 90% Jumbo mortgage loans:**

Fixed rates: Terms available for 10, 15, 20, and 30 years

ARM rates: 3/1, 5/1, 7/1, and 10/1 Libor ARMs

PMI options include borrower paid monthly, single premium, split premium or Lender paid PMI (with rate adjustment)

Seller may pay up to 6% of the purchase price towards buyers closing costs

Often the jumbo loan borrower’s CPA and/or financial advisor will advise their clients as part of a financial strategy to put down less money on their home and keep their money for reserves and in their investments.
