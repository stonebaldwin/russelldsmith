## Search for VA Approved Condos

As a Veteran or active [military buyer](http://www.military.com/), the simple [condo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Condominium) life may be appealing.  Being able to own a [property](https://www.zillow.com/) that has very little upkeep plus amenities is a positive for the busy military life.  Plus, condos may provide a lower stress [retirement](http://www.aarp.org/) life.  But in order for a VA approved buyer to purchase a condo, it must be VA approved.  So, when a VA buyer starts the [home search](http://www.brunswickfinehomes.com/) process, make sure to check the VA approved condo list for your desired area.  We actually have a very popular article “[Searching for VA Approved Condos](/blog/va-approved-condo-search/)” which explains how to navigate the VA site.  It can be a tricky system, so we recommend reading our article before going straight to the VA site.  For instance, it is always recommended to search by city, county, and partial name separately.
