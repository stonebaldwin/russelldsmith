## Just Released South Carolina USDA Income Limits

[**USDA**](/usda-loans/) Guaranteed Rural Housing Home Loans are an excellent choice for not only first time buyers but ![Charleston South Carolina USDA Income Limits](/images/blog/south-carolina-usda-income-limits/charleston-sc-300x286.gif)move-up buyers too.  One of the biggest roadblocks to homeownership today is lack of down payment.  But USDA loans give buyers a great no down payment option.  Plus it offers a 30 year fixed interest rate.  USDA is not as popular as [**FHA,**](/fha-loans/) but most of the time USDA is actually the better option because of the lower monthly PMI (called [**Annual Fee**](/blog/2016-2017-usda-funding-fee-and-annual-fee-decrease-to-lower-mortgage-payments/)).  Plus USDA does not require a down payment, where FHA has a minimum 3.5% down payment requirement.  Russell D Smith can help buyers in qualifying including if they meet the South Carolina USDA Income Limits.

In order to qualify for a USDA loan, there are two primary requirements besides the normal credit, debt to income, and asset qualifications.  First, properties must be in a USDA Eligible Area.  Check out our [**Step by Step Guide to Locate USDA Eligible Properties**](/blog/step-by-step-instructions-for-locating-usda-eligible-properties-areas/) to find eligible homes in your area.  Secondly, the total household income cannot exceed the USDA income limits for the county or area of the subject property.  In this article, we show the South Carolina USDA Income Limits.

## County Household Income Limits

The USDA income limits are based on the household income of everyone that will live in the property that ![Columbia South Carolina USDA Elgible Areas](/images/blog/south-carolina-usda-income-limits/columbia-sc-300x227.gif)is 18 or older.  So household members that are not on the mortgage loan application are even included in the maximum limit.

Almost all SC counties have the standard USDA Income Limits.  For households of one to four members, the income limit is $75,650.  In addition, if the household is five or more people, the income limit is $99,850.  But, there are some counties in South Carolina that are higher than the standard limits as shown below in the chart.
