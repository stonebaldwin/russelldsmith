## **Employment & Industry is Alive in Lee County**

Lee County has historically experienced right at the national average unemployment rate and ![Employment Opportunities in Moore County NC](/images/blog/sanford-nc/employment_content-300x235.png)additionally you can live within the county and have a short drive to neighboring county opportunities.  Most of the workforce within the county fall under the manufacturing industry and when the great recession hit many of these jobs were lost.  However the economy is moving once again in Lee County and Manufacturing is making a huge comeback here.  Additionally, many Lee County residents also are able to live here while having a short commute to work in neighboring Fayetteville, Fort Bragg, RDU, and Pinehurst.

The city of Sanford has made it a priority to build Sanford’s infrastructure while not sacrificing the small town, family atmosphere.  Today Sanford and Lee County are undergoing a $15 million downtown streetscape and revitalization program as well as extending miles of greenways and sidewalks. Making Sanford a pedestrian friendly place that appeals to shoppers and tourists adding to the enjoyment of its local citizens.  Lee County also has passed a $23 million County and Education bond as well that will be adding to its award winning Central Carolina Community College campus, creating jobs in healthcare and trades, expanding our Dennis Wicker Civic Center into a fully modernized Business and Convention Center adding 14,000 square feet including plans for a downtown Business Incubator

## Places to Eat and Lots of Activities for Residents & Visitors

Culture and a small town atmosphere combine to create an experience that makes you see why the 60,000 residents call Sanford NC and Lee County home.

**Dining Hot Spots – Southern Cooking & Cuisine at its Best**

If you’re looking for a great steak, try out [**Davison’s Steaks**](http://www.davisonssteaks.com/lunch-menu.html), the #1 ranked restaurant by TripAdvisor.com or [**The Flame Steakhouse**](http://www.theflamesteakhouse.com/) which also offers favorites such as prime rib, crab cakes, and a great salad bar.  Sanford has some ![best places to eat in Sanford NC](/images/blog/sanford-nc/variety-food-plates-300x300.jpg)great true southern food choices:  [**Stubbs & Son BBQ**](http://stubbsandsonbbq.com/) is known for eastern NC style chopped barbecue and they have freshly cut French fries that can’t be beat.  [**The Steele Pig**](http://www.thesteelepig.com/) offers what they describe as southern food at its best including a “15 oz bone-in, smoked & grilled Filet Mignon or simply the best house-made burger you can get”.  Want brisket, collard greens, barbecue, live music, and a beer?  Try [**Smoke & Barrel**](http://smokeandbarrelsanford.com/) where their mission is “to be your neighborhood spot for great food and drink, live music, and plenty of good cheer”.  The **Fairview Dairy Bar** is as one customer called it “A great casual spot for burgers, ice cream, fried pickles, and breakfast.  Also, experience Chef Hamm’s famous creations at [**Café 121**](http://www.chefhamm.com/) where they invite you to “Pull up a chair and sit a spell”.

**Things to do –** **Indoor or Outdoor Activities**

Sanford NC and Lee County have plenty things to do so you can have a Staycation and the area takes great pride in the ![Jordan Lake in Lee County NC](/images/blog/sanford-nc/jordan-lake-rec-area-300x95.jpg)culture provided for its residents and visitors.  Spending a day at the Jordan Lake State Recreation Area on a sunny day would be very relaxing.  Experience performing arts at the historic Temple Theatre which is a 1920’s vaudeville venue and they even offer training for aspiring theatre artists if you have dreamed of being on stage.  Put on your calendar to attend the Sanford NC Arts & Vine Festival which is a 2 day celebration of art from local artists and some of the state’s leading brewers and vineyards.  Drink it all in and experience this unique experience for your senses!

Of course, don’t forget GOLF!  Sanford is also know for its proximity to Pinehurst but is home to Tobacco Road Golf Course  – a perennial top 10 public course by every golfing publication in the country.  Locally owned and developed from the former Lee Paving Company’s sandpits it is truly spectacular.  Not to mention our own C_arolina Trace Country Club boasts two 18-hole golf courses designed by famed golf course architect Robert Trent Jones, Sr. Both offer championship golf with vistas of Lake Trace and soaring Carolina pines._

If golf is not for you just trek 15 minute to the 117 miles of shoreline at B. Everett Jordan Lake for camping, boating, hiking and family time. Also, visit the Endor Iron Furnace or head East 12 miles to Raven Rock State Park. Go mountain biking or canoeing  at nearby San Lee Park.  For Shooting enthusiasts check out Deep River Sporting Clays  in Northern Lee County. Award winning Orvis endorsed Wing Shooting facility. Sanford and Lee County may be small but it does not lack for things to do downtown or throughout the County.
