VA loans may actually be the best home loan in the USA!  Heck, even the world possibly!  Although most [VA loans](/va-loans/) are used for a no down payment purchase, it is also a very beneficial solution to buy a higher priced home.  Each county in the U.S. has a [VA loan limit](/blog/va-loan-limits/).  The good news is that VA will still offer this great loan while exceeding these county limits.  This is called a VA jumbo loan.  On these loans, a VA jumbo loan down payment is required and must be calculated.  So let’s discuss the benefits of a VA jumbo loan and a little known trick for obtaining the down payment.

## **VA Jumbo Loan Down Payment Option**


A VA jumbo loan is tough to beat for qualifying buyers in the luxury home market!  The [Veteran’s Administration](https://www.va.gov/) follows the Fannie Mae conforming loan size limits for each county.  So just like conforming loans, when the limit is exceeded it goes into the jumbo arena.  Although, [VA jumbo loans](/blog/va-jumbo-loans-nc-sc/) are different and it is more complicated for the buyer to figure a down payment.  The good thing is a seasoned VA loan officer is able to determine down payments using a formula specified by VA.  There are several wonderful benefits of these loans for Veteran’s, [service members](http://www.military.com/), or qualified surviving spouses.

## **Potential VA Jumbo Benefits**

-   Up to [$1,000,000 loan amount](/jumbo-loans/) allowed
-   No monthly PMI – Keeps payment lower in comparison to other low down payment jumbo loans
-   Often lower down payment than traditional jumbo loans
-   May be more flexible on credit & debt ratios than traditional jumbo loans
-   [Liberal seller paid costs and concessions](/blog/closing-costs-sales-concessions/).

But there is one more potential benefit of a VA jumbo loan that most do not know!  VA jumbo loans require a down ![va jumbo loan down payment](/images/blog/va-jumbo-loan-down-payment/benefits-copy-300x191.jpg)payment.  Although, it could be a small percentage down.  The down payment depends on the borrower’s available entitlement and purchase price of the new [home](http://www.realtor.com/).  Good News!  [VA](https://www.va.gov/) will allow for another source to cover all or a portion of the down payment.  This amount could actually cover up to 4% of the sales price.  Imagine if your VA purchase required a 3% down payment, then this solution could cover it for you.  Additionally, the seller could pay all of the buyer’s customary closing costs.  How about that for some benefits?

## Ideas for VA Jumbo Loan Down Payment

There are a lot of ways to come up with down payment and a VA jumbo loan down payment is no different.  A few ideas include the following:

-   Of course, checking and savings accounts
-   Borrow against an asset such as a car, CD, retirement account
-   Sale of another home
-   Home equity line of credit advance on another property
-   Gift funds
-   Income bonuses or commissions
