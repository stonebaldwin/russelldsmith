## How do I find out if a Wake County property address is USDA eligible?

**First View the Area:**  Unless you have a specific property already in mind, it is best to search the general USDA eligible areas first.  Then you know the boundaries.  In order to view these areas and zoom into street level, follow these easy instructions:

[Zoom In to Find USDA Eligible Areas](<https://eligibility.sc.egov.usda.gov/eligibility/welcomeAction.do?pageAction=sfp&NavKey=property@11′ link_target=>)

**Step 1: Select “Single Family Housing Guaranteed”**

![usda loans wilmington nc](/images/blog/usda-loans-raleigh-nc/usda-single-family.png)

**2: Use your mouse to move your area to the middle of the screen**

**3:  Use the scroll bar on the map or your mouse scroll button**

Browse the area, especially if the area you are searching is on the borderline of being eligible or not. Really pay attention to which side of a particular road you need to stay on for instance.

**Next, View the Specific Property Address**:  Once you have zoomed into the eligible areas, you could type in actual property addresses.  So if you found a home for sale on [Zillow](https://www.zillow.com/), [Trulia](https://www.trulia.com/), [Homes](http://www.homes.com/), or [Realtor](http://www.realtor.com/), you can type in the address.  The site will tell you if the property is eligible.
