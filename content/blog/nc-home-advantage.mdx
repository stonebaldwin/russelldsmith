One of the most popular questions we hear is what kind of programs are there with 100% financing and the answer has been [VA](/va-loans/) and [USDA](/usda-loans/).  But now, NC Housing Finance Agency has the NC Home Advantage Down Payment Assistance program.  NC first time or move up buyers may finance 100% or more financing when purchasing a primary residence in North Carolina.  The Down Payment Assistance is a _**zero interest, zero payment loan that is forgiven after 15 years**_!

#### **NC Home Advantage with Down Payment Assistance Details:**

-   For [VA](/va-loans/), [USDA](/usda-loans/), and [FHA](/fha-loans/) government loans, the _Down Payment Assistance is allowed to be up to 5%_ of the first mortgage amount!
    -   Especially helpful on [FHA](/fha-loans/) loans where the Down Payment Assistance can cover the down payment and even go
        
        ![Down payment assistance available for NC buyers at 0%](/images/blog/nc-home-advantage/couple-in-front-of-house-with-keys-300x199.jpg)
        
        Down payment assistance available for NC buyers at 0%
        
        towards closing costs
        
-   For conventional loans at 97%, the _Down Payment Assistance is allowed up to 3%_ which gives the buyer right at 100% financing
    -   The monthly PMI is much lower than normal conventional PMI
    -   Down payment assistance could be used towards paying the PMI at once
    -   Great option for conventional buyers that have a credit score between 640 & 700 with normally a lower rate than regular conventional rates
-   Applicants can make up to $85,000 (not based on household income like [USDA](/usda-loans/))
-   Does not have territorial restraints such as [USDA](/usda-loans/) (except the property must be in NC)
-   Can be used with the MCC Tax Credit if a qualifying first time buyer.  [See the details for the MCC tax credit](/blog/mortgage-credit-certificate/)

**Examples of allowed Down Payment Assistance:**

-   [FHA loan](/fha-loans/) of 96.5% financing and Down Payment Assistance of 5% to cover the 3.5% down payment and the remaining 1.5% towards closing costs
-   Conventional loan of 97% and Down Payment Assistance of 3% of the first mortgage amount, gives the buyer right at 100% financing.  Then the seller can pay up to 3% of the price in closing costs for the buyer
-   [VA](/va-loans/) or [USDA](/usda-loans/) loan which will finance 100% of the sales price and the Down Payment Assistance can go towards paying closing costs
