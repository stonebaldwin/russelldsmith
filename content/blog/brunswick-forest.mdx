Brunswick Forest is its own town within a town located in Leland, Brunswick County, NC.  Brunswick Forest boasts a relaxing and active way of life for young professionals, growing families, to retirees.  There is an abundance of home types and prices, low property taxes, quick access to area beaches, and about anything you can think of to do in the sun when you choose Brunswick Forest as your new home.  When buying a home, you hear the term “Location, Location, Location”, and Brunswick Forest has it all.  The following are some details to help you start considering if Brunswick Forest could be your New Home:
