When looking to buy, **[build](/blog/va-construction-loan/)**, or refinance a home using a **[VA loan,](/blog/va-loan-down-payment/)** a VA appraisal must be performed. Although, the exception is that an appraisal is not required on a **[VA Interest Rate Reduction Refinance Loan](/blog/va-refinance-irrrl/)** (VA IRRRL). The purposes of this VA valuation report are to determine the value, condition, and safety of the home. Thus, lenders, the Department of Veteran Affairs, and even Veterans depend heavily on this key information. But, a common question Veterans ask involves the amount of the VA appraisal fees. In order to keep costs affordable, VA sets a cap which VA appraisers may charge and it varies regionally.

Additionally, no lender may up-charge the appraiser’s fee. Whatever the actual charge is, the lender must only pass that along to borrowers. Even borrowers who pay the lender up-front must receive a refund if the actual cost comes in lower.

## Maximum VA Appraisal Fees Vary by Property Type

There are four main types of appraisal forms involved in VA loans.

-   Single Family (1004 Appraisal)
-   Condominium (1073/1075 Appraisal)
-   Manufactured Homes (1004C Appraisal)
-   2 – 4 Unit Multi Family (1025 Appraisal)

Because of specific requirements involved with condos, manufactured homes, and multi family properties, the VA appraisal fees may be higher when compared to traditional single family homes. Depending on the area and appraiser, the increase could be $0 – $200 higher. Multi family units are the most expensive.

## VA Appraisal Cancelled Orders

Sometimes, various situations require buyers or lenders to cancel an appraisal order. For the fortunate few, it is prior to any performed work. Thus, there is no fee and anything paid up-front is refunded by the lender. Although, once a portion of the work has been performed, the VA appraiser may charge for their work.

In order to cancel a VA appraisal, lenders must notify the assigned appraiser and the applicable VA Regional Loan Center. The following is an example of maximum fees for cancelled orders:

-   Work completed prior to appointment – $50
-   Appointment made, notes in VA system – $175
-   Review of the home interior – 50% of posted fee
-   Over 50% of work completed, report must be completed – full fee

## Additional VA Appraisal Fees Allowed

Occasionally, there are circumstances which put additional work on an appraiser. VA realizes this and will allow cases of extra fees to cover these areas. Although, any extra fees must be approved by the applicable VA Regional Loan Center prior to inspection. Examples of possible extra VA appraisal fees include…

-   Complex properties
-   Excessive mileage to property
-   Unusual circumstances (weather, unpassable roads, islands)
-   Extra transportation requirements (boat, air, specialty vehicles)
-   Additional work required for final inspection of new construction \*\*

\*\* On new construction, a final inspection fee is allowed up to typically $150. But, if additional work is needed during the final inspection, an extra $50 could be approved. So, let’s get into the state maximum VA appraisal fees.
