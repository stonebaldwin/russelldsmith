## Key Statistics for Topsail Reef Condos:

-   **Property tax rates**:  Property taxes are affordable.  The Onslow County property tax rate is .675 and North Topsail Tax Rate is .3932.  These are based on 2015 rates.
-   **HOA dues**:  Dues vary based on the size and location of each unit at roughly a range of $200 – $400
-   **Insurance**:  Master insurance policies cover the building from fire, storm, and flood.  So mortgages will required a “walls in condo policy” also called an H06 policy
-   **Flood Zone.**  Obviously, Topsail Reef is in a flood hazard area.  So request a copy of the flood insurance from the HOA.
