## 2016 Awards Breakfast for Real Estate Agents and Staff

Thursday morning, C21 The Real Estate Center held their annual Appreciation and Awards breakfast at Something New at Abigail’s in Lumberton, NC.  Elliott Williamson, owner and broker in charge, led the proceedings.  He stressed how the agents really go out of their way to help each other as they are family, strive to help their communities, are very active in local organizations, and are determined to give the best service to their clients.  You could tell the sense of pride that Elliott has in his group.  We all got to enjoy a good southern breakfast complete with eggs, bacon, sausage, grits, biscuits, coffee, and sweet tea.  Yum!

Most of the C21 agents and staff were able to attend the event as well as **Russell Smith with Russell Smith** and Will Baker with MetLife Insurance.

## Sales Statistics for Century 21 and Local Market
